Why Framer

Design and publish without compromise.

From first layout to live URL in minutes. Framer's visual canvas directly outputs production HTML/CSS — no export, no handoff, no compromise on quality.

✁
Visual-first canvas

Draw layouts, add components, and see your site exactly as visitors will. No code required — but full code access when you need it.

🚀
Publish instantly

One click deploys to Framer's global CDN with custom domains, SSL, and 99.9% uptime. From canvas to live in under 30 seconds.

💫
Interactions built-in

Scroll animations, hover effects, and page transitions. Design motion directly on the canvas without writing a line of animation code.

🤖
AI components

Describe a component and Framer AI builds it. Edit the result visually or dive into the code. The fastest way from idea to component.

🌐
CMS & Localization

Connect to your CMS and publish in 40+ languages. Dynamic pages, smart collections, and localized content — all in one canvas.

🌍
Figma integration

Import directly from Figma. Your design tokens, components, and layouts arrive in Framer ready to make interactive and publish.
