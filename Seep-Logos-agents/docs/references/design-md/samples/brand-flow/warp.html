Platform Features

A terminal that
thinks with you.

⚡

Blocks, not lines

Commands and their output are grouped into blocks — select, copy, share, or rerun any block instantly. Stop scrolling through walls of text.

🧠

AI command search

Type what you want in plain English — Warp translates it to the right command. Powered by AI, trained on thousands of real-world terminal workflows.

📄

Input editor

A proper text editor inside your terminal. Cut, paste, multi-cursor, syntax highlighting — write complex commands without fighting with readline.

🍀

Workflows & notebooks

Save command sequences as shareable Notebooks. Onboard teammates, document runbooks, and share your most-used commands with your team.

Warp AI

Command palette for everything you need to do.

Hit ⌘K to open the AI command palette. Search for any command, action, or Warp feature — or just describe what you want to happen in plain English.
